# Human Subject Protection Plan

Required future controls include understandable consent, right to withdraw, confidentiality, data minimization, incident management, compensation policy, vulnerable subject protections, physician availability, and continuous communication.

Human use remains blocked until signed evidence exists for physical HIL, preclinical safety, risk control verification, clinical protocol, REB/IRB approval, Health Canada ITA or FDA IDE authorization, and independent medical signoff.
