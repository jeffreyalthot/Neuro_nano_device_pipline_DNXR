# Valuation Assumptions and Boundaries

> Confidentiality: Level 3 — NDA controlled unless otherwise stated.
> Status: Due diligence scaffold for preclinical R&D software evidence.
> Boundary: Preclinical R&D software evidence package; simulation/HIL readiness scaffold; not authorized for human use; not clinically validated; not a certified medical device.


Valuation discussions may consider architecture, implementation volume, QMS organization, tests, traceability, safety gates, documentation, and integration potential. Valuation must not assume clinical validation, regulatory approval, certified medical-device status, or human-use readiness.
