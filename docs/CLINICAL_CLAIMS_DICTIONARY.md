# Clinical Claims Dictionary

Allowed claim: simulation and preclinical preparation only. Forbidden claims without signed external authorization: safe for human use, clinical test ready, implant ready, medical approved, full consciousness transfer, guaranteed full dive, and human full-dive immersion.

Human use remains blocked until signed evidence exists for physical HIL, preclinical safety, risk control verification, clinical protocol, REB/IRB approval, Health Canada ITA or FDA IDE authorization, and independent medical signoff.
