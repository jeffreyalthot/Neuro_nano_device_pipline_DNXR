# Patent FTO Question List

> Confidentiality: Level 4 — NDA controlled, need-to-know distribution.
> Evidence boundary: declarations are diligence records for counsel/owner completion and do not themselves transfer rights or authorize human use.


## Counsel questions
1. Which implemented mechanisms may be patentable, and who owns them?
2. Which third-party patents could read on neural interface, full-dive, HIL, replay, safety-gate, or hardware-bench workflows?
3. Are there publication, sale, demonstration, or disclosure dates that affect patentability?
4. Do any open-source licenses impose patent grants or retaliation clauses?
5. What rights, if any, are proposed for transfer, license, escrow, or exclusivity?

## Evidence to attach
- Version/hash of disclosed source archive.
- Prior disclosure log.
- Contributor/originality declaration.
- Technical summary and architecture diagrams.
- Exclusion statement for clinical/human-use readiness claims.
