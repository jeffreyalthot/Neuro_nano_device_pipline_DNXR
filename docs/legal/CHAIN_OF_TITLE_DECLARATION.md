# Chain of Title Declaration

> Confidentiality: Level 4 — NDA controlled, need-to-know distribution.
> Evidence boundary: declarations are diligence records for counsel/owner completion and do not themselves transfer rights or authorize human use.


## Purpose
Document the ownership trail for source code, documentation, generated QMS artifacts, test evidence, data-room archives, and related commercial rights. This record must be completed and signed by the authorized owner and reviewed by counsel before external reliance.

## Ownership assertions to complete
| Item | Required evidence | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Repository origin | Initial creation date, author/owner identity, repository history | Open | Attach export or commit history summary |
| Contributions | Contributor list, employment/assignment status, inbound licenses | Open | Identify any non-owner contributors |
| Generated artifacts | Tool/script provenance and reproducibility method | Open | Link to QMS generation scripts and logs |
| Third-party material | License, source URL/vendor, redistribution rights | Open | Cross-reference third-party register |
| Prior disclosures | Date, recipient, material version/hash, NDA status | Open | Cross-reference prior disclosure log |
| Commercial transfer scope | Assets, rights, exclusions, warranties, support commitments | Open | Cross-reference transfer checklist |

## Clinical/human-use limitation
No chain-of-title record conveys clinical authorization, IDE/ITA approval, IRB/REB approval, physician signoff, or human-subject permission. Those remain controlled by QMS/regulatory evidence gates.
