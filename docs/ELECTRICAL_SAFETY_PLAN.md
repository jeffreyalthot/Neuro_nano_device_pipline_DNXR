# Electrical Safety Plan

Future hardware work must address IEC 60601-1 electrical safety, isolation, leakage current, thermal limits, alarms, EMC under IEC 60601-1-2, watchdog cutoff, and independent stimulus power removal.

## Version 0.4.9 — Clinical Lockdown + Real Evidence Gate

Affirmation autorisée: **Plateforme logicielle de simulation et de préparation préclinique pour revue médicale; non autorisée pour test humain.**

Human use remains blocked until signed evidence exists for physical HIL, preclinical safety, risk control verification, clinical protocol, REB/IRB approval, Health Canada ITA or FDA IDE authorization, and independent medical signoff.
